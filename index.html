Day 1 Time-Weighting Principle

Because money is paid periodically, earlier payments compound significantly longer than later ones. Your very first premium works for the entire tenure, while your last premium barely earns any interest. This tool accounts for every single period your cash sits in the account.

🔬

Calculation Methodology

Bank FD: Each installment → FD at P×(1+r/n)^(n×t). Interest taxed at slab rate.
Insurance IRR: Newton-Raphson solver for annualized rate equating all outflows to maturity inflow.
MF SIP: Each installment grows at expected return. Tax: Equity LTCG 12.5% on gains > ₹1.25L; Debt at slab rate.
LIC Tax: Maturity tax-free under Sec 10(10D) if annual premium ≤ 10% of sum assured.
